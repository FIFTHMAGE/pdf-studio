What you can do with it

Everything happens on one screen. Pick a tool from the top bar and work right on the page.

Annotate
Highlight text, draw on the page, or leave a note for someone.
Change text and images
Cover up a typo or an old logo, then type or drop in the replacement.
Merge and reorder pages
Combine several files, drag pages into order, rotate them, or pull out the ones you need.
Fill in forms and sign
Type straight into form fields, then draw or type your signature and put it where it goes.
Turn photos into a PDF
Add a few JPGs or PNGs, like scanned receipts, and save them as one file.
Your files stay with you
PDF Studio runs in your browser, so the documents you open are never sent to a server.
